# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Project Ledger serves one owner managing a small collection of coding projects. They use it as a working dashboard to quickly understand project tooling, recurring spend, optional Stripe revenue, reminders, and supporting project knowledge without maintaining a heavier business system.

## Product Purpose

Keep the operational and financial details of personal coding projects in one dependable place. Success means the owner can enter, find, compare, and reorganize information quickly, with totals that reconcile and records that remain easy to maintain over time.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, approachable. The interface should feel trustworthy and practical, with inviting language and enough warmth to make routine upkeep pleasant.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-style dashboard decoration, attention-seeking motion, inflated SaaS presentation, unfamiliar controls, and unnecessary workflow complexity. The product should not resemble a landing page or turn simple personal record-keeping into enterprise administration.

## Design Principles

- Put the current task first and let interface chrome recede.
- Make financial and relationship state explicit, dependable, and easy to verify.
- Prefer familiar controls and progressive disclosure over custom interaction patterns.
- Keep maintenance lightweight with inviting empty states and concise language.
- Add personality through thoughtful details, not decoration or extra steps.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, strong text and control contrast, reduced-motion preferences, and non-color cues for state. Drag interactions must retain an accessible non-pointer path.
